Eleven days, five countries, one group tour: Budapest, a Slovakia-and-Vienna day trip, Athens, a speedboat out to Hydra, Santorini, and a last stop in London before flying home. Hotels, food, and the moments worth knowing about if you're planning the same route.

**Hotels:** Novotel Budapest City · Acropolis View Hotel (Athens) · Cosmopolitan Suites (Santorini) · Hilton London Bankside

### Day 1 — April 2, 2026 — Departure

**Route:** JFK → Belgrade (Nikola Tesla Airport) → Budapest

- Departed JFK on Air Serbia
- Long layover at Nikola Tesla Airport, Serbia
- Connecting flight on to Budapest

> Air Serbia's transfer process was smooth, but the layover was long — a good excuse to rest before the itinerary picks up.

![JFK departure](assets/images/greece-europe-tour-2026/01-jfk-departure.jpg)

**Stay:** Novotel Budapest City

- Checked into the hotel
- Evening Danube river cruise with a welcome drink — night views of the Hungarian Parliament
- Dinner spread across Rin Thai Restaurant, The Bros, and a local gyro spot
- Delayed luggage finally caught up with us

### Day 4 — April 5, 2026 — Slovakia & Vienna Day Trip

**Route:** Budapest → Bratislava → Vienna → Budapest

**Bratislava, Slovakia:** Bratislava Castle · Old Town · St. Michael's Gate · St. Martin's Cathedral · Ecco Mall

**Vienna, Austria:** lunch at In-Dish Indian Restaurant · Hofburg Palace · Burg Theatre · Austrian Parliament

> Old Town Bratislava is compact and easy to walk end to end. Vienna's architecture rewards slowing down — don't rush the Hofburg.

### Day 5 — April 6, 2026 — Budapest to Athens

- Breakfast at the Novotel — a huge seating area, a fresh-juice station, and a spread big enough for the whole group
- Bus transfer to the airport, then Aegean Airlines to Athens
- Priority Pass lounge access made the wait easy
- Checked into Acropolis View Hotel, ordered pizza, and called it a night

![Novotel breakfast spread](assets/images/greece-europe-tour-2026/07-novotel-breakfast.jpg)

### Day 6 — April 7, 2026 — Athens Exploration

- Rooftop breakfast with a straight-on view of the Acropolis, every morning
- Personal walking tour of the Acropolis and the Acropolis Museum
- Walked the markets and Plaka
- Lunch: vegetarian pasta, stuffed vegetables, lamb, bread, and Greek yogurt
- Hot crepes and ice cream from a local shop
- A sunset viewpoint — crowded

> Sunset spots in Athens get packed fast. Locals point to Filopappou Hill as the quieter alternative — worth researching before you go, and on the list for next time.

### Day 7 — April 8, 2026 — Hydra & Poros Islands

- Bus, then a high-speed boat to Hydra — about an hour
- Brunch at EN PLO (margherita pizza), ice cream at Sousami Bakery
- Walked the port and the alleys; some of the group rode mules
- Attempted the hike toward the flag viewpoint — easy to lose the trail, but there are plenty of spots along the way to swim, dive, and just sit by the water
- Lunch at Πιάτο Restaurant
- Pit stop in Poros on the boat back to Athens

> Be careful with the mule vendors on Hydra — confirm the price and the number of mules included before you pay. It's an easy spot to get scammed on both.

### Day 8 — April 9, 2026 — Athens to Santorini

- Flight from Athens to Santorini on Aegean Airlines
- Burger King at the airport before boarding — the plant-based nuggets were genuinely excellent, worth seeking out anywhere in Europe
- Skyserve lounge at the airport
- Taxi from the Santorini airport to the hotel (~€39)
- Checked into Cosmopolitan Suites — highly recommend
- Explored Fira: fresh gyros for €4, a standout falafel gyro, crepes and ice cream again
- Local shopping

> Double-check labels before buying clothes as souvenirs — not everything sold as Greek-made actually is.

![Fira at sunset](assets/images/greece-europe-tour-2026/12-fira-sunset.jpg)

### Day 9 — April 10, 2026 — Santorini Tour

**Bus tour:** Pyrgos Kallistis Monastery (the island's highest point) · Perivolos Black Sand Beach · the Three Domes in Oia

**Lunch:** Momos Restaurant

**Evening:** sunset in Oia, then the Blue Dome Church for the night candle view

### Day 10 — April 11, 2026 — Santorini to London

- French toast for breakfast, enjoyed from the balcony
- Last-minute shopping in town — worth comparing prices across shops before buying
- Taxi to the airport, flight to London on British Airways
- Heathrow Express to Paddington, then a short ride to Hilton London Bankside
- Dinner at KUTIR — lamb shank and chicken tikka, an excellent meal

> Overweight luggage fees can be steep — we saw close to €80. If your ticket was booked through a different airline than the one you're flying, double-check their baggage and check-in rules in advance; partner carriers don't always align.

![Balcony views, Santorini](assets/images/greece-europe-tour-2026/15-santorini-balcony.jpg)

### Day 11 — April 12, 2026 — London & Return

- Breakfast at the Hilton Bankside — a custom omelet station, fresh juices, pastries, and standout service
- Walked to Big Ben and the London Eye; a marathon was running through the city that morning
- Train to Heathrow, then American Airlines back to the US — good food, good service, landed home safely

### Final Notes

Hydra and Santorini were the standouts — an island detour and a sunset town that both earned the hype. Budapest was a better base than expected, and London closed things out well: the Hilton Bankside breakfast alone is worth the stop. Culture, food, and island time, back to back, across five countries in eleven days.
