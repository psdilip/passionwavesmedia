Five days covering a wide stretch of Ontario: downtown Toronto, a full day in Niagara, the Kitchener-Waterloo-Cambridge tri-cities, and a stop in London to see family friends.

### Day 1

![Toronto Pearson International Airport terminal](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1545323112-0689f24b5601?w=1600&q=80&fm=jpg&fit=crop)
*Toronto Pearson International Airport · Photo by [Lumi W](https://unsplash.com/@lumiwh) on [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com)*

**Activity:**
- Flew into Toronto Pearson International Airport. Fast, well-organized process with a lot of the check-in and customs handled through kiosks.
- On the way back at the end of the trip, the Global Entry lane made re-entry quick. As long as you're honest and within the limits on what you're bringing back into the country, it's a smooth process.

### Day 2

![Toronto skyline with the CN Tower, seen from Toronto Island](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1697607932663-71d59867b733?w=1600&q=80&fm=jpg&fit=crop)
*Toronto skyline from Toronto Island · Photo by [Satya N K](https://unsplash.com/@satyank) on [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com)*

**Meals:** Lunch at Pai Northern Thai Kitchen · dinner at El Catrin

**Activity:**
- Drove from the suburbs into downtown Toronto.
- **The Well**: a large mixed-use retail development, great architecture and design, worth a walk-through even if you're not buying anything.
- **St. Lawrence Market**: a multi-story market with fresh meat, cheese, fruit, olives, spices, fresh pasta, and a wide range of cultural food stalls. Easy to spend a lot of time (and money) here.
- **Pai Northern Thai Kitchen**: great interior, gets busy, book ahead or go early. Got the pad Thai and fried rice, both excellent, and it's known for good Thai tea.
- Walked to the **Jack Layton Ferry Terminal** and took the ferry to Toronto Island.
- Rented bikes on the island. It happened to be white blossom season, a whole stretch of trees in bloom, great for pictures.
- Stopped at the **Sunfish Cut viewpoint**, one of the best skyline shots of the CN Tower you'll get from anywhere near the city.
- Ferried back to the mainland and drove to the **Distillery Winter Village**: cool restaurants, string lighting, good architecture, a great atmosphere especially if you're there during the festive season.
- **El Catrin** for dinner: guacamole made tableside, fish tacos, and more. Watching the guacamole get made fresh at the table was a nice touch.

### Day 3

![Horseshoe Falls at Niagara Falls, with a tour boat approaching the mist](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1463695970743-ae65cca05743?w=1600&q=80&fm=jpg&fit=crop)
*Horseshoe Falls, Niagara Falls · Photo by [Kalen Emsley](https://unsplash.com/@kalenemsley) on [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com)*

**Meals:** Cookies at Budapest Bakeshop · dinner at Hakkalicious

**Activity:**
- Stopped at FreshCo to see how a Canadian grocery store compares, and at Tim Hortons on the way out of town.
- **Wayne Gretzky Estates**: a winery worth visiting, but it gets crowded, go early to get seating. There's a backyard area with cornhole and other games, a genuinely good hangout spot even outside of the wine tasting.
- **Niagara-on-the-Lake**: a beautiful street lined with shops, bakeries, and clothing stores, plus a lot of flowers and greenery. An easy, pretty walk.
- **Budapest Bakeshop** on Queen Street: the cookies are excellent, worth the stop on their own.
- **Niagara Falls**: found parking a bit of a walk from the falls themselves, near an open plaza area. Worth the walk.
- **Journey Behind the Falls**: a genuinely different way to experience the falls, side-by-side instead of the usual top-down view, including a tunnel where you can see the water rushing past in front of you.
- **Hakkalicious** for dinner: Indo-Chinese food, chicken lollipops, noodles, fried rice. Everything we ordered was excellent, a must-try if you're in the area.

### Day 4

![University of Waterloo's Arts building facade](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1745272701378-4408ee919a76?w=1600&q=80&fm=jpg&fit=crop)
*University of Waterloo · Photo by [Allen Y](https://unsplash.com/@yanahd) on [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com)*

**Meals:** Tim Hortons · boba at University Shops Plaza

**Activity:**
- Drove to London, Ontario to visit family friends and just hung out for the day.
- Stopped at Tim Hortons on the way back.
- Walked around the **University of Waterloo** campus, a genuinely impressive place, known for computer science and known for working on some cutting-edge robotics research. Worth a walk-through even without a campus tour booked.
- Got boba at University Shops Plaza before heading back.

### Day 5

![A canoe on a calm, tree-lined river](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1691197672191-d07124391453?w=1600&q=80&fm=jpg&fit=crop)
*Photo by [Ariana Kaminski](https://unsplash.com/@arianka) on [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com)*

**Meals:** Dinner at Sri Dakshin Indian Vegetarian Restaurant

**Activity:**
- Rented kayaks at **Stanley Park Optimist Natural Area** in Kitchener and spent two to three hours out on the Grand River.
- Drove to **Sri Dakshin Indian Vegetarian Restaurant** in Cambridge for dinner: fresh dosas, a genuinely good pongal, and rose milk to finish. A must-try if you're in the tri-cities area.
- Headed home after dinner to close out the trip.
